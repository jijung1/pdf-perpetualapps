Name & icon options

Six directions, in priority order: basic PDF editing first, then private, pay once, no ads. Icons are shown the way Android crops them: only the centre 72 of the 108 dp canvas is visible, inside a circle, squircle or rounded square depending on the phone.

Store titles avoid “free”, “no ads”, “buy once” and similar words on purpose. Google Play's metadata policy doesn't allow price or promotional claims in the title or icon. Those messages go in the short description, screenshots and the full description. Short descriptions here are ≤ 80 characters and titles ≤ 30.
