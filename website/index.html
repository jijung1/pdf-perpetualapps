Questions

How much does it cost?

US$6.99 in the United States, paid once. In other countries, Google Play shows the price in your local currency, and taxes may apply depending on where you live. There's no subscription, and no in-app purchases or ads. Purchases and refunds are handled by Google Play.

Will the app keep getting updates?

You keep the version you bought, and it doesn't depend on any server or account. Future updates and support aren't guaranteed, and the app may stop being updated or supported at some point.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Everything happens on your phone. The app doesn't even have permission to go online.

Does whiteout really remove the text?

Yes. When you save, text, image pixels and drawings under the box are deleted from the file, not just covered. Before saving, the app outlines exactly which characters will go. For sensitive documents, open the saved copy and check it before you share it.

Are signatures legally binding?

Signatures and initials are pictures of your handwriting placed on the page. They aren't certificate-based digital signatures, and the app doesn't verify who signed. Whether a signed PDF is accepted depends on who you send it to.

What happens to form fields when I save?

Saving flattens form fields and comments into the page, so the PDF looks the same in every app. The saved copy can't be filled in again as a form. Your original file is kept.

Can it open password-protected PDFs?

No. The app tells you clearly when a file is password-protected.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 8.0 or newer. Adding HEIC photos needs Android 9 or newer.
